One Stop Centre – Support for Women

Information page to understand available support services

What is a One Stop Centre

A One Stop Centre (OSC) provides free and confidential support to women affected by violence or distress.

The centre works in coordination with government departments to ensure safety, medical assistance, legal aid, and counseling.

Services Available

  • Medical assistance
  • Police coordination
  • Legal support
  • Counseling services
  • Temporary support (as applicable)

Who Can Approach

  • Women facing violence or distress
  • Family members or well-wishers
  • Referred cases (police / hospital)
  • Walk-in support available
